🌧️ Nilwala River Basin Rainfall Analysis Maps
This repository presents a curated collection of thematic maps developed as part of a university research project.
These maps support the spatial and temporal analysis of rainfall extremes and hydrological patterns within the Nilwala River Basin.

🗺️ Maps Included
📊 combined_data_R95pTOT
Combined rainfall data exceeding the 95th percentile threshold.

📊 combined_data_R99pTOT
Combined rainfall data exceeding the 99th percentile threshold.

⛰️ Elevation Map of the Nilwala River Basin
Topographic representation highlighting elevation gradients across the basin.

🧭 Location Map of the Nilwala River Basin – Base Map
Geographical base layout providing spatial context.

📍 Location Map of the Nilwala River Basin
Detailed administrative and geographical location reference.

🌧️ max_Rx5Day
Maximum recorded 5-day cumulative rainfall.

📅 Mean R9 Days (1995–2024)
Average number of days per year with rainfall above the 9th percentile from 1995 to 2024.

📅 Mean R99 Days (1995–2024)
Average number of days per year with rainfall above the 99th percentile from 1995 to 2024.

☔ Mean RX1day (1995–2024)
Average maximum 1-day rainfall over the study period.

☔ Mean RX5day (1995–2024)
Average maximum 5-day rainfall from 1995 to 2024.

☔ Mean RXtday (1995–2024)
Average maximum rainfall over a defined threshold period within the study years.

📈 R95pTOT
Annual total rainfall exceeding the 95th percentile.

📈 R99pTOT
Annual total rainfall exceeding the 99th percentile.

📅 RX1day 2016
Maximum 1-day rainfall for the year 2016.

☔ RX1Day
General dataset of maximum 1-day rainfall events.

🎯 Purpose
These maps aim to identify, visualize, and interpret extreme rainfall patterns across the Nilwala River Basin. 

They serve as a valuable resource for:

  Hydrological and climatological research

  Flood risk assessment

  Long-term climate change impact analysis

Water resource and disaster management planning
