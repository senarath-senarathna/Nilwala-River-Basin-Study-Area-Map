Nilwala River Basin Rainfall Analysis Maps
This repository contains a collection of maps created for a university research project focused on the "Assessment of Extreme Rainfall Indices to Identify Extreme Rainfall in Nilwala Basin, Sri Lanka." The maps are designed to support the analysis of rainfall patterns and extreme weather events in the region.


Maps Included
combined_data_R95pTOT: Combined rainfall data exceeding the 95th percentile threshold.
combined_data_R99pTOT: Combined rainfall data exceeding the 99th percentile threshold.
Elevation Map of the Nilwala River Basin: Topographical representation of the basin's elevation.
Location Map of the Nilwala River Basin - Base Map: Base geographical layout of the Nilwala River Basin.
Location Map of the Nilwala River Basin: Detailed location map of the basin.
max_Rx5Day: Maximum 5-day rainfall totals.
Mean R9 Days (1995-2024): Average number of days with rainfall exceeding the 9th percentile over the period 1995-2024.
Mean R99 Days (1995-2024): Average number of days with rainfall exceeding the 99th percentile over the period 1995-2024.
Mean RX1day (1995-2024): Average maximum 1-day rainfall over the period 1995-2024.
Mean RX5day (1995-2024): Average maximum 5-day rainfall over the period 1995-2024.
Mean RXtday (1995-2024): Average maximum rainfall over a threshold period from 1995-2024.
R95pTOT: Rainfall totals exceeding the 95th percentile.
R99pTOT: Rainfall totals exceeding the 99th percentile.
RX1day 2016: Maximum 1-day rainfall for the year 2016.
RX1Day: Maximum 1-day rainfall data.



Purpose
These maps are intended to assist in identifying and analyzing extreme rainfall events in the Nilwala River Basin, providing valuable data for research and climate studies.
