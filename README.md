Nilwala River Basin Rainfall Analysis Maps

This repository contains a collection of maps created for a university research project.
The maps support the analysis of extreme rainfall patterns and hydrological variability in the region.


Maps Included :

[•] combined_data_R95pTOT - 
Combined rainfall data exceeding the 95th percentile threshold.

[•] combined_data_R99pTOT - 
Combined rainfall data exceeding the 99th percentile threshold.

[•] Elevation Map of the Nilwala River Basin - 
Topographical representation of the basin's elevation.

[•] Location Map of the Nilwala River Basin – Base Map - 
Base geographical layout of the Nilwala River Basin.

[•] Location Map of the Nilwala River Basin - 
Detailed administrative and geographical location map.

[•] max_Rx5Day - 
Maximum 5-day cumulative rainfall totals.

[•] Mean R9 Days (1995–2024) - 
Average number of days per year with rainfall exceeding the 9th percentile from 1995–2024.

[•] Mean R99 Days (1995–2024) - 
Average number of days per year with rainfall exceeding the 99th percentile from 1995–2024.

[•] Mean RX1day (1995–2024) - 
Average maximum 1-day rainfall during the study period.

[•] Mean RX5day (1995–2024) - 
Average maximum 5-day rainfall from 1995–2024.

[•] Mean RXtday (1995–2024) - 
Average maximum rainfall over a defined threshold period.

[•] R95pTOT - 
Annual rainfall totals exceeding the 95th percentile.

[•] R99pTOT - 
Annual rainfall totals exceeding the 99th percentile.

[•] RX1day 2016 - 
Maximum 1-day rainfall for the year 2016.

[•] RX1Day - 
General dataset of maximum 1-day rainfall values.




Purpose

These maps are intended to assist in identifying and analyzing extreme rainfall events in the Nilwala River Basin. 

They provide essential data for:

[•] Research in climate science and hydrology

[•] Flood risk and disaster preparedness

[•] Environmental impact assessments

[•] Long-term water resource planning
